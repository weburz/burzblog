# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique decisions and code, not
people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and publishing another
person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may moderate contributions and restrict participation for harmful
conduct. A confidential reporting contact and appeals process are pending
maintainer selection before inviting a public community. Do not post sensitive
reports in public issues. This policy is original project text, not an imported
third-party code of conduct.
